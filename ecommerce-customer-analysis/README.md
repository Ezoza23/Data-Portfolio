# E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Analysis

## Business Problem
An e-commerce company wants to understand customer purchasing behavior
in order to increase revenue and retain high-value customers.

## Key Business Questions
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- What purchasing patterns exist over time?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How can customer retention be improved?

## Dataset
UCI Online Retail Dataset

## Tools
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Key Insights
- Revenue shows strong seasonality, with peaks toward the end of the year, indicating increased holiday demand.
- A small group of high-value (VIP) customers contributes a disproportionately large share of total revenue.
- A significant number of customers make only one purchase, highlighting a customer retention opportunity.
- Certain products consistently rank among top revenue generators, making them critical for inventory planning.
- Customers with recent inactivity represent a potential churn risk.

## Recommendations
- Implement a loyalty program for high-value (VIP) customers by offering exclusive discounts, early access to products, or reward points to increase retention and lifetime value.
- Launch targeted re-engagement campaigns for inactive customers, such as personalized email promotions or limited-time discounts, to reduce churn.
- Focus marketing efforts on repeat customers by recommending products related to their previous purchases.
- Optimize inventory management by prioritizing top-performing products that consistently generate the highest revenue.
- Monitor customer purchasing frequency and recency regularly to identify at-risk customers early and take proactive retention actions.
